💼 Featured Projects

Employee Login & Attendance Monitoring System

Python Database Security

Context: During work-from-home scenarios, employees may log in from unauthorized locations such as vacation spots, affecting productivity and attendance authenticity.

Problem: Traditional login systems verify only credentials and do not validate employee work location.

Solution: Developed a system that tracks login/logout time, calculates working hours, and restricts login access if the employee is not present at an approved base location such as home or office.

Features: User Registration, Login/Logout Tracking, Working Hours Calculation, Attendance Monitoring, Location-based Login Restriction.

Decentralized Carpooling Application Using Blockchain

Blockchain Smart Contracts Web3

Context: Existing carpooling platforms face issues related to trust, fake profiles, and lack of transparency.

Problem: Users often hesitate to share rides due to unclear ride history and centralized data handling.

Solution: Conceptualized a blockchain-based decentralized carpooling system that maintains immutable ride history and trust records between users.

Features: Ride Matching, Transparent Ride Records, Blockchain-based Trust Model, Peer-to-Peer Interaction.
